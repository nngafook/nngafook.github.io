# Privacy Policy for Sixfold

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Sixfold is a single-player puzzle game developed by Nicholas Ng-A-Fook.

## Information the app collects

Sixfold collects no personal information. The app has no user accounts, no
sign-in, and no network connectivity. It does not contain advertising or
analytics software, and it transmits nothing off your device.

## Information shared with third parties

None. Because no information is collected, none is shared, sold, or disclosed.

## Permissions

Sixfold requests no Android permissions beyond those the system grants every
app by default.

## Children's privacy

The app collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Distribution platforms

Google Play and Apple's App Store collect their own data when you download or
install an app, independent of this app. Their handling of that data is
governed by their own privacy policies:

- Google Play: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Apple App Store: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
